HOW TO PLAY

  • Each phase is a zone of the facility. Clean it to 80% to unlock the next phase.
  • Vacuum debris into your tank, then empty it at a green Disposal Chute for credits.
  • Hydro Lance washes sludge stains. Spore Torch burns spore pods. Refill at Service Stations.
  • The grime breeds creatures — the dirtier the zone, the faster they come. Kills pay credits.
  • Spend credits at the Supply Terminal, wall-buys, perk machines and the Refinery.
